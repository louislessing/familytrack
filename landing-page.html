Everything You Need in One Place

FamilyTrack brings together all the tools you need for successful co-parenting and family management.

Children Profiles

Comprehensive profiles for each child including medical history, school information, allergies, emergency contacts, and developmental milestones all in one secure location.

Shared Calendar

Coordinate custody schedules, appointments, school events, and activities. Real-time synchronization ensures everyone stays informed and on the same page.

Handover Tracking

Document custody exchanges with timestamps, locations, photo verification, and notes. Create a clear record for transparency and legal purposes.

Expense Management

Track child-related expenses across 16+ categories including education, medical, clothing, activities, and more. Generate reports and split costs fairly.

Communication Log

Keep a detailed record of all co-parent communications. Searchable, timestamped entries that can be exported for legal or mediation purposes.

Document Storage

Securely store and share important documents: court orders, medical records, school reports, immunization records, and legal agreements in one encrypted vault.

Guardian Management

Support for multiple carer types including parents, guardians, foster carers, and kinship carers. Manage permissions and access levels for each role.

Legal Records

Track court orders, custody agreements, compliance deadlines, and legal milestones. Receive automated reminders for important dates and requirements.

Clothing Inventory

Track children's clothing across households. Know what's at each location, what needs replacing, and avoid duplicate purchases. Perfect for seasonal transitions.

LAC Reviews

Comprehensive tracking for Looked After Children reviews. Manage statutory requirements, review schedules, care plans, and compliance documentation.

Secure & Private

Bank-level encryption, GDPR compliant data handling, and role-based access controls ensure your family's sensitive information stays private and secure.

Mobile Accessible

Access FamilyTrack from any device - desktop, tablet, or smartphone. Fully responsive design ensures a seamless experience wherever you are.
